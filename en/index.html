Remove ads with a donation ❤️

Lulu Kids is built by one person. The only way to remove ads is to donate through PayPal or Ko-fi: you then receive a code and enter it in the app. For grown-ups only.

  1. Before donating: open Info in the app and check that there is a Donor code field. If it is missing, update the app on Google Play; if it is still missing, your version does not accept codes yet.
  2. Donate with PayPal or Ko-fi (buttons below).
  3. Email nescool101@gmail.com with the app version (bottom of the Info screen) and your receipt (or the name and email you donated with).
  4. We check the donation and reply with your code. It is sent by hand, usually within a few days.
  5. In the app: Info → grown-up question → Donor code → Remove ads.

Ads stay removed when the app updates. If you reinstall or change device, email us again with the version. Please do not post your code.
